## 泛美桃李国际舞蹈节圆满落幕

亲爱的舞蹈爱好者们:

随着夏日最后一缕夕阳温柔地洒落在珠海的海平面上，为期一周的泛美桃李国际舞蹈节暨国际青少年艺术周，也在无数欢笑与掌声中缓缓落下了帷幕。

这不仅仅是一场舞蹈的盛宴，更是一次心灵的碰撞与文化的交融，让我们共同回顾这七天里，那些闪耀的瞬间与难忘的回忆吧！

## 7月16日-22日

当艺术周的序曲在珠海悠扬响起，来自五湖四海的舞蹈精灵们汇聚一堂，用他们的热情与才华，点燃了这座城市的每一个角落。从古典到现代，从民族到国际，每一种舞步都承载着舞者的梦想与坚持，每一场表演都是对美的极致追求。

![泛美桃李國際舞蹈節活動照片](../images/news/festival-2024/photo-02.jpg)

## 7月17日-19日

舞蹈节的高潮如约而至。舞台上，灯光璀璨，音乐激昂，选手们身着华服，翩翩起舞，将每一个动作都演绎得淋漓尽致。而台下，世界各地的权威专家教授们以专业的眼光和温暖的鼓励，为每一位舞者提供了宝贵的点评与建议，让这场舞蹈盛宴更加意义非凡。

## The Pan America Taoli International Dance Festival Comes to a Successful Close

Dear dance lovers,

As the last rays of the summer sun gently fell across the sea in Zhuhai, the week-long Pan America Taoli International Dance Festival and International Youth Arts Week drew to a close amid laughter and applause.

This was more than a celebration of dance: it was a meeting of hearts and cultures. Let us look back at the shining moments and unforgettable memories of these seven days!

## July 16–22

![Taoli International Dance Festival](../images/news/festival-2024/photo-01.jpg)

As Arts Week opened in Zhuhai, dancers from near and far gathered, bringing their enthusiasm and talent to every corner of the city. From classical to contemporary, from folk traditions to international styles, every step carried the dancers’ dreams and dedication, and every performance expressed a pursuit of beauty.

![Taoli International Dance Festival](../images/news/festival-2024/photo-02.jpg)

## July 17–19

The festival reached its much-anticipated high point. Under dazzling lights and stirring music, competitors in beautiful costumes brought every movement to life. Offstage, distinguished experts and professors from around the world offered valuable feedback and advice with professional insight and warm encouragement, making the festival even more meaningful.
